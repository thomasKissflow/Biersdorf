# 12 - Questions for BDF: tech, platform and non-functional lens

Discovery only; nothing is being built. Prepared 2026-10-05 for call 1 with BDF's consultant (business side, relays to the German client team), with items parked for call 2 and for written follow-up.

**Lens.** Supplier identity and access, BDF single sign-on, environments, hosting and data residency, integrations (even if post-MVP), files and virus scanning, retention and audit-proof archiving, AI-supported review, languages and accessibility, volumes and performance, reporting, and what the MVP test environment must contain. Every question is phrased so the consultant can relay it, carries our default, and is tied to BDF's own documents.

**Sources.** Read for this file: `00-glossary.md`, source notes 01-07, analysis 01-06, the raw dumps (quoted phrases verified against them), and the Kissflow builder manual (`~/Downloads/master.md` §0, §2, §3.4-3.9, §4.6-4.8, §6.1, §14, §15). Citation codes follow `00-glossary.md`. VQ rows are Excel rows of the non-functional questionnaire (r16 = Authentication / SSO, r20 = Data protection, r23 = Archiving and retention, and so on).

**How to use.**
- Ask the **Top 12** first. Everything marked *Call 1* is suitable for the consultant; *Call 2* usually needs BDF IT, data protection or a department expert; *Written* can go in a follow-up e-mail.
- **Our hypothesis** is the default we propose if BDF has no answer. Say it out loud: it shows we have thought it through and speeds up the decision.
- **KB overlap** names the Knowledge Baseline item (KB OP-xx, NEW-xx, BD-xx ...) we move forward instead of repeating. The KB's answer columns are all empty, so we never re-ask a KB item verbatim; we offer a concrete option or default.
- The table columns are client-safe. Kissflow-internal reasoning is in the last section only.

**Counts.** 44 questions in 12 themes. Impact: critical 6, high 26, medium 11, low 1. Best call: call 1 24, call 2 15, written 5.
