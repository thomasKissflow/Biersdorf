## Full content digest

### Process model expressed by the matrix (stated)
1. **PS-00:** Business administrators maintain the template, field types, the versioned control-table rule set, the role model and section-to-department responsibility [BRM r2–r6].
2. **PS-01:** RMM creates the CIQ project manually [r7].
3. **PS-02:** The supplier sees **only** the basic data and configuration questions [r8]. Mandatory check [r9]. Data is stored structured [r10]. Metadata stays changeable until PS-11 [r11].
4. **PS-03:** The system derives the scope from the rule set, sorts each part into mandatory / optional / N.A., and hides N.A. parts from the supplier. Departments may extend the scope with a justification. If metadata changes, the scope is re-derived without silent data loss [r12–r16].
5. **PS-04:** The supplier completes the CIQ, with context-bound uploads, a completeness check and justification for missing items. Several supplier contacts can work in parallel [r17–r19].
6. **PS-05:** The supplier **confirms completeness** (end of part 1). This is not an approval. The submitted version is frozen as the review version, and the affected departments are notified automatically and receive tasks [r20–r23].
7. **PS-06:** Departments review in parallel. Every internal role can read everything but edit only its own area. There is an internal-only communication area. RMES documentation, RMES pre-population and AI document review are "MVP partial scope" [r24–r29].
8. **PS-07–PS-09:** Clarification loop. Each query is tied to an object and becomes a supplier task with a responsible person and due date. Additional CIQ parts can be activated at any time. Open points carry open / in progress / closed (traffic light) and the loop can repeat without limit. **All open points block completion** [r30–r39].
9. **PS-10:** Each department completes its subprocess. This is "not a business approval". Safety and Regulatory complete separately, and delegation must be auditable. The supplier receives **one** notification once **all** applicable subprocesses are complete [r40–r42].
10. **PS-11:** The **supplier grants the final CIQ release**; the CIQ is then frozen [r43–r44].
11. **PS-12:** The project completes automatically. A PDF summary is generated, versioned immutably, filed at the project and e-mailed to the RMM owner. Cancellation by RMM is possible [r45–r47].
12. **Cross-cutting:** RBAC with supplier/internal separation, every action as a task, status at three levels with dashboards, an immutable audit trail, and notifications [r48–r52].

The matrix contains **no Beiersdorf approve/reject decision on the raw material anywhere**. REQ-PROC-015, -038 and -014 explicitly say that none of the confirmations is a BDF raw-material approval [r20, r21, r40].
