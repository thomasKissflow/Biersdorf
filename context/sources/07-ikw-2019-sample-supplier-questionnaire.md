**Layout conventions** (verified on rendered pages):
- Two-column tables (question | answer).
- Most questions are "Yes / No" with an italic "If Yes, please specify" free-text cell.
- Substance lists are tables with a ppm column. Metals have an extra "Method/LOQ" column.
- Repeated identity blocks are printed a fixed number of times (Additive 1–4, Component A–D) instead of as a variable-length table.

## Full content digest

### Explanations [IKW p.2]
- IKW developed the questionnaire with member companies. It helps cosmetics manufacturers obtain the information needed to ensure final-product quality and meet regulatory requirements, and it may help identify areas for improvement.
- It is "a basis for discussion" to be "handled in a flexible manner". Companies may adapt it by deletions or additions. It is especially useful where the supplier's own documentation is insufficient.
- **Reconfirmation:** the questionnaire "should be regularly reconfirmed (e.g. in two year intervals)" to include new scientific findings, studies and legal requirements.
- **Proactive updates:** suppliers should inform customers "automatically and without delay" about new developments affecting the contents.
- **Scope per form:** "formulated for raw materials containing one main component. For multicomponent systems the corresponding pages can be copied."
- **Legal basis:** EU perspective. It is based on Cosmetics Regulation (EC) 1223/2009 for human-health aspects and REACH (EC) 1907/2006 for environmental issues. Some international aspects are in Annex II.
- Liability disclaimer (no liability except intent or gross negligence).

### 2 Manufacturing [IKW p.4–6]
- **Origin of starting materials.** Each is a separate Yes/No, so several can apply: Animal; Plant ("for plant extracts see Annex I"); **Microorganism**; Petrochemical; Mineral; Other. Then "If YES, please specify" [p.4].
- **Access and benefit-sharing** (for animal-, plant- or microorganism-derived materials) [p.4]:
  - In scope of **Regulation (EU) No. 511/2014**? Yes / No. If No, list reasons (e.g. species or activities exempted). If Yes, is compliance with 511/2014 / **Nagoya Protocol** ensured and documented? Yes / No.
  - Within scope of the **provider country's** ABS laws? Yes / No. If No, reasons. If Yes, compliance ensured and documented? Yes / No.
- **GMO** [p.5]: derived from genetically modified organisms? Yes / No. If Yes, is the supplied material free from detectable GMO DNA? Yes / No.
- **Animal origin** [p.5]: species; country of origin; body parts used; compliance with **Reg. (EC) 1069/2009** (animal by-products; replaces 1774/2002) and Reg. (EC) 1223/2009 (**Annex II/419**); "Please attach **BSE/TSE certificate**".
- **Plant origin** [p.5]: plants from sustained cultivation of plantations? Yes / No; species; country of origin; plant parts used.
- **CITES** [p.5]: species listed in CITES (WA)? Yes / No. If Yes, attach the import permission from the appropriate authorities.
- **Powders, dispersions or aerosols containing solid particles** [p.5]: particle size distribution (range); volume-specific surface area; fraction of particles (unbound, aggregates or agglomerates) with one or more external dimensions in 1–100 nm (**% number** and **% mass**); intentionally manufactured nanomaterial? Yes / No; nanomaterial per **EC Recommendation 2011/696/EU**? Yes / No; nanomaterial per Reg. (EC) 1223/2009? Yes / No.
- **Quartz-containing products** [p.6]: does the particle size distribution reach the alveolar fraction < 10 µm? Yes / No.
- **Manufacturing process** [p.6]: starting materials; solvent(s); catalyst(s); description.
- **Purification process** [p.6]: additive(s); description.

### 5 Microbiological Status [IKW p.10–11]
- **"Do you accept a hygiene audit?"** Yes / No [p.10].
- **Antimicrobial treatment**, each Yes / No: gamma irradiation; beta irradiation; moist heat; dry heat; ethylene oxide; electron beam; other (specify) [p.10].
- **Microbiological specification:** total viable count of bacteria and fungi (**target < 10 cfu/g**). Test method used, each Yes / No: DAB/EP (German / European Pharmacopoeia); USP; ISO method (specify); other (specify) [p.10].
- **If the target cannot be fulfilled:** total viable count < 100 cfu/g? Yes / No; is it a natural ingredient? Yes / No; comments [p.11].
- **Absence of pathogenic microorganisms** (confirm), each Yes / No: *Candida albicans*, *Escherichia coli*, *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*, *Staphylococcus aureus*, others (specify); comments [p.11].
- **Preservative challenge test** of a freshly prepared **and an aged** production sample: conducted? Yes / No; if Yes, test method; comments [p.11].

### 6 Storage Recommendations [IKW p.12]
Recommended storage temperature; description of temperature effects; storage under special conditions (Yes / No + specify); **Shelf life** row with columns "**Recertification date**" / "At temperature of" / "Time"; **storage class**; **dust explosion class**; comments.

### 7 Toxicology [IKW p.12–13]
Header: "Information on toxicology data (according to 'The **SCCS Notes of Guidance** for the Testing of Cosmetic Ingredients and their Safety Evaluation **10th Revision**') is enclosed". Each item is Yes / No, meaning data enclosed:
1 Acute toxicity: oral · 2 Acute toxicity: dermal · 3 Acute toxicity: inhalation · 4 Skin irritation · 5 Mucous membrane (eye) irritation · 6 Skin sensitisation potential · 7 Chronic or subacute toxicity · 8 Carcinogenicity · 9 Reproductive toxicity (fertility and teratogenicity) · 10 Genotoxicity · 11 Dermal absorption / penetration · 12 Photoirritation · 13 Photosensitisation · 14 Toxicokinetics (absorption, distribution, metabolism, elimination) · 15 Any data on human experience (allergies, skin irritation, sensitisation) + specify · 16 Other in vitro or in vivo studies + specify · 17 Any other tests or studies + specify.

### 8 Ecotoxicology Data [IKW p.13–14]
"Enclosed information on ecotoxicology data", each Yes / No:
- Biodegradability per **OECD 301 (A–F) / OECD 302 B**
- Bioaccumulation potential (e.g. octanol/water partition coefficient or BCF fish)
- Water solubility
- Aquatic toxicity: algae; daphnia; bacteria
- Aquatic chronic toxicity: fish
- **Water hazard class (WGK, Germany)** + specify
- Additional information

### Animal Testing, unnumbered (expected chapter 9) [IKW p.14]
- Relates to **Art. 11.2(e) of Reg. (EC) 1223/2009**. "Please only make entries if the testing was performed **after 11 September 2004**."
- Animal testing carried out in developing this cosmetic raw material? Yes / No. If Yes: date and type of test; country where performed.
- Year of last *in vivo* testing.
- **Purpose of the study**, each Yes / No: REACH (EC) 1907/2006; non-EU chemical legislation; EU cosmetic legislation (76/768/EEC) / Reg. (EC) 1223/2009; non-EU cosmetic legislation; other (specify).
- Comments.

### 10 Regulatory Information [IKW p.15]
- Any possible import / export restrictions applicable for the EU? Yes / No.
- Does the supplier confirm REACH compliance? Yes / No.
- Any ingredients on the ECHA **Candidate List (SVHC)**? Yes / No + specify.
- **Quality/Safety standards**, each Yes / No: manufactured per **EFfCI-GMP** Standard for Cosmetic Ingredients; complies with **IFS HPC** Standard; complies with **food standards (ISO 22001 [sic], HACCP etc.)** + specify; other + specify.
- **Intellectual Property:** is the raw material a patented product of the manufacturer? Yes / No; additional information (e.g. licence agreement).

### Annex I: Plant Extracts [IKW p.16–18]
- Plant name; scientific name; plant part(s) used; **risk of adulteration with other plants**; place of cultivation (country/region); method of harvesting/collection; period of harvesting; stage of harvesting; pre-treatment of plant material.
- Purification process (description); extraction method (description); solvent(s) (type and amount).
- Is the plant extract **screened for pesticides**? Yes / No.
- Contains **denatured alcohol**? Yes / No. If Yes, specify the denaturants.
- Physico-chemical parameters; description of control and QA measures applied to the starting plant material, process and final product.
- **Absence of toxicologically relevant natural secondary components (pyrrolizidine alkaloids etc.)** Yes / No; comments.
- **Description of the extract (main components, active ingredients): Component A to Component D**, each with IUPAC; INCI (EU); INCI (US); CAS; EINECS/ELINCS; concentration (-range) [% w/w]; molecular weight; and a "biological / toxicological profile of action for active ingredients": desired effect; mechanism of action; evidence of action; profile of side effects.

### Annex II: International / Non-EU Aspects [IKW p.18]
- Ingredients or impurities listed under **California Proposition 65**? Yes / No.
- Ingredients declarable under **California Senate Bill 484**? Yes / No.
- Ingredients classified as **VOC** under US VOC regulations? Yes / No.
- **Registration / notification under chemical legislation**, each Yes / No: US; Canada; China; Japan; Korea; Australia; Taiwan; other (specify).
- **Taxation:** contains a VOC subject to the Swiss "Ordinance on the Incentive Tax on Volatile Organic Compounds" (VOCV, AS 2008 1765)? Yes / No.

### Annex III: Specific Requirements [IKW p.19]
Kosher Yes / No; Halal Yes / No; **Vegan** Yes / No; comments.

### Build-relevant patterns in the IKW template (inferred)
- **"Yes/No + if Yes, specify"** is the dominant pattern, which maps to conditional fields. BDF's FLM uses the same pattern (e.g. FLD-A1-201 → -202/-203).
- **Fixed repeated blocks** (Additive 1–4, Component A–D) are a paper limitation; digitally they become repeatable tables. This matches BDF's old FLD-2.1-008 [Feldkatalog r24].
- **Pre-defined substance lists with value columns** (metals with Method/LOQ and ppm; 26 allergens) map to tables with seeded rows, numeric values, units and optional limit checks [ReqDoc p.13 REQ-RMES-008].
- **"Data enclosed" Yes/No per endpoint** (tox and ecotox) maps to checkbox → mandatory upload, as in BDF's old "CORE RULE" [Feldkatalog r41].
- **Document checklist** ("Please enclose") maps to K1 mandatory documents.

## Notable details, gaps & internal inconsistencies
1. **Chapter 9 is unnumbered.** "Animal Testing" has no number; numbering goes 8 → 10 [IKW p.14] (verified on the rendered page).
2. **N-Vinyl-2-Pyrrolidone appears twice**, under "Monomers" and under "Nitrogen compounds or nitrosating agents" [IKW p.8].
3. **Typos:** "Ehylene oxide", "Dioxines" [IKW p.8]; "Does the particle size distribution reaches…" [IKW p.6]; "ISO 22001" is probably ISO 22000 (inferred) [IKW p.15]; "10 th Revision" [IKW p.12]; the file name has "Beiserdorf".
4. **"Purification process – Additive(s)"** is an ambiguous label: additives used in purification, or present after it? [IKW p.6].
5. **"Recertification date" sits under "Shelf life"** with "At temperature of / Time" columns. The table layout is ambiguous about whether this is a re-test date or a shelf-life statement [IKW p.12].
6. **Toxicology and ecotoxicology Yes/No means "data enclosed"**, not a test result [IKW p.12, p.13]. A digital form must label this unambiguously (inferred).
7. **Scope limitation:** one main component per form; multi-component products need copied pages [IKW p.2]. Additives are capped at 4 blocks and extract components at 4 (A–D) [IKW p.6–7, p.17–18].
8. **Old legal references:** the 76/768/EEC cosmetics directive appears next to 1223/2009 [IKW p.14]. Reg. 1774/2002 is noted as replaced by 1069/2009 [IKW p.5].
9. **Currency of content:** it is the 2019 edition. The substance lists (e.g. the 26 fragrance allergens) reflect the 2019 rules. EU allergen labelling was later extended, so the lists should be read as structure, not current content (inferred; external knowledge, verify).
10. **Units:** impurities are in ppm [IKW p.7–9]; BDF's old limits are in mg/kg [Feldkatalog r27–r29]. These are equivalent for solids (inferred), but the app needs one unit convention.
11. **No sign-off block:** the IKW template has no signature, date or responsible-person fields in the extracted text (verified). BDF's old catalogue had a "Verification" block and digital signature [Feldkatalog r10–r15], which the new version dropped.
