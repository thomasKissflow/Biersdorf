### 2b. Found and fixed tonight: the RMES record time

- **The bug:** live, Kissflow's form list rows carry no `_created_at` or `_modified_at`, only the business fields and `_id`. I proved it on the RMES forms. The app reads the RMES forms by list only, so the RMES tab never showed when the record was created. The test fake keeps those keys, so no offline test caught it.
- **The fix:** the time now comes from the record's own creation history row, which the app already loads. The tab says "Record created (its time is not in the case history)" when that row is missing. The probe above proves it on live data.

### 2c. Same root cause elsewhere (older, not fixed tonight; 4 task chips)

A read-only audit found other places that read those keys from list rows. They predate the RMES work and touch shared code, so they wait for your review:
- **Medium: history actors.** A Beiersdorf user who appears only in history can show as "Beiersdorf user", or a history line as "System". This is limited by the colleague directory, which names the members of the CIQ roles.
- **Low: configuration.** With a duplicated singleton key (OWN, REM or SET:current), the screens and the writer pick different rows.
- **Cosmetic: data consoles.** The app's and the portal's data consoles show an empty Modified column.
- **Test-only: integrity suite.** It can't detect a changed form row live.
