- **Every 050313 since 7 Oct is at a step without one:** the spike case (then read as "a limit of the key"), the 20 Oct open-point walk, and tonight's open-point edit and close and the query answer.
- **Ruled out:** the field permissions (Editable) and the flow grants (identical on Request and Query).

**The portal session proved the other half live tonight** (its memory note "user route: section permission decides"). Where the section IS Editable (CIQP Request fill-in), the user route also wrote fields that are **ReadOnly** at that step: App_Case_Item_Id, Release_Flag, Request_Locked, Case_Phase_Label.
- **So the section's step permission decides, and per-field step permissions are ignored on the user route** (which the SDK's updateItem uses). The admin route is not affected.
- **REST submits ignore Mandatory**; the screens already enforce Mandatory themselves.
- **The portal session's pass 2 reproduced it 42 times**: at keep, give, view and respond, and at all six open-point department steps.
  - At fill-in, the ReadOnly fields leaked in 10 of 10 runs.
  - The Uploads child table was refused at keep even though it is Editable there and sits outside the Details section.
