How the CIQ is modelled in Kissflow

The CIQ runs in two connected areas of Kissflow: a supplier portal for external suppliers, and the CIQ Digital app for Beiersdorf. This page shows the modules in each area, what each one does, and how they connect.

Two areas, one case

Supplier portal

For external suppliers only

  • One role: Supplier.
  • Holds only the supplier's own tasks: fill in the CIQ, answer questions, give the final release.
  • A supplier sees only their own cases.
CIQ Digital app

For Beiersdorf users only

  • 11 roles: the RMM, five department reviewers, Customs & Trade Compliance, Legal / IP, Business administrator, Auditor and Business owner (reports).
  • Every Beiersdorf decision is a step here: reviews, questions to the supplier, the case itself.
  • Holds the supplier master, the questionnaire and rules versions, notes and the audit trail.

Every portal item has a twin in the app, with the same case number. That keeps the supplier's side and Beiersdorf's side of one case together.

The Kissflow building blocks we use

ProcessA record that moves through steps. Each step has an assignee and can have a deadline.
FormA data record with no steps, such as the supplier master or a note.
Child tableRows inside one record, such as a supplier's contacts or a request's uploaded files.
ListThe values of a dropdown, such as case phases or departments.
RoleA group of people. Permissions and step assignments are given to roles.
IntegrationAn automation: when something happens, do something, such as send an e-mail.
ReportA saved view of a process or form, with columns, filters and charts, shared with roles.
SharingSettings that let the app and the portal read each other's data.

What each module does

Supplier portal

ModuleTypeWhat it doesConnected to
CIQP Request Process

The supplier's CIQ, one per case. The supplier fills in the whole questionnaire in one task. They upload files and confirm completeness. Afterwards the request stays with them, so they can follow the case to the end.

Steps: Issue request → Supplier: fill in the CIQ → Supplier: keep the CIQ up to date → Supplier: give the final release → End. The first two supplier steps go to the supplier contacts named on the case. The release step goes only to the contacts allowed to confirm.

Final release step: it's reached once every department review is complete, and every answer is read-only there, so Kissflow itself locks the questionnaire. If Beiersdorf reopens a review at that point, a go-to step sends the request back to "keep the CIQ up to date".

Deadlines: "fill in the CIQ" is due on the supplier due date, with a reminder 7 days before and an overdue reminder the day after. The release is due within 3 days.

It holds the answers, which sections apply, the requested documents, and a child table of uploaded files.

 Its twin, CIQ Case, with the same case number.
CIQP Query Process One item per round of a question from Beiersdorf. The supplier answers with text or a document, or says they cannot provide it. The answer is due on the question's due date, with a reminder 2 days before and an overdue reminder the day after. Its twin, CIQ Open Point. Each round is a new query.
CIQP Help Article Form The help pages suppliers see in the portal. Suppliers can only read them. Stands alone.

CIQ Digital app

ModuleTypeWhat it doesConnected to
CIQ Case Process

The backbone: one item per case, started by the RMM. It holds the material, the supplier and contacts, the due date and the pinned questionnaire version. It also holds the case phase: Supplier filling in the CIQ · Review · Awaiting final release · Completed · Cancelled.

Steps: Create case → Waiting for supplier: CIQ → Beiersdorf review and final release → End.

 Every other module, through its case number.
CIQ Review Process

One item per department review: Regulatory Affairs, Product Safety, Quality Management, Microbiology, Sustainability and the RMM. Each department has its own step, assigned to its role.

Because every review is its own item, a review can start late, be reopened or be reassigned without touching the others.

 CIQ Case. Raises CIQ Open Points.
CIQ Open Point Process One item per question or document request from a department. It's assigned to the person who raised it, so only they can accept, return, edit or close it. The full thread is kept for the final document. CIQ Case, and its twin CIQP Query in the portal.
CIQ Supplier Form The supplier master: legal name, DUNS, country, and a child table of contacts with each contact's e-mail and whether they may confirm. The Business administrator maintains it. Copied onto each CIQ Case when it's created.
CIQ Config Version Form The questionnaire and the rules as versioned data, one row per version. A case keeps the version it started with, so a later change never alters running cases. CIQ Case, which pins its version.
CIQ Scope Run Form A snapshot of which sections apply, taken at completeness and whenever the scope changes later. CIQ Case.
CIQ RMES Safety · CIQ RMES Regulatory Form The department's assessment criteria, results and findings. Only the own department sees them. The department's decision goes on its review. CIQ Case, through a Kissflow reference field.
CIQ Internal Note Form Internal notes and @mentions on a case or a section. Suppliers never see them. CIQ Case.
CIQ Audit Event Form The case history and the audit trail: who did what, and when. CIQ Case.
E-mail integration Integration A Kissflow integration that sends e-mails through a webhook when something happens in the portal or the app, for example when a question is raised or answered. No e-mail data is stored. Events in the portal and the app. Sends to suppliers and Beiersdorf users.
Lists List Dropdown values: case phase, review status, priority, departments, open point kind and outcome, and more. Used by the fields above.

One case, end to end

  1. The RMM creates the case. They pick the supplier and its contacts, and the case pins the current questionnaire version.CIQ Case · CIQ Supplier · CIQ Config Version
  2. The supplier gets the CIQ in the portal. They fill it in, with sections opening as they answer, upload documents and confirm completeness.CIQP Request
  3. The departments review in parallel, each on its own review. Safety and Regulatory record their RMES assessment.CIQ Review · CIQ RMES Safety · CIQ RMES Regulatory
  4. Questions go to the supplier and the answers come back, round by round.CIQ Open Point ⇔ CIQP Query
  5. When every review is complete, the request moves to its final release step. The questionnaire locks, and the supplier gives the final release.CIQP Request · final release step
  6. The case completes, with its full history on record.CIQ Case · CIQ Audit Event

How it's configured in Kissflow

What you'll see in the Kissflow builder during the demo.

Roles, and people added by e-mail

  • Portal: one role, Supplier. Supplier contacts are invited to the portal by e-mail and given that role.
  • App, 11 roles: Raw Material Manager (RMM); the Regulatory Affairs, Product Safety, Quality Management, Microbiology and Sustainability reviewers; Customs & Trade Compliance; Legal / IP; Business administrator; Auditor; Business owner (reports).
  • People are added to a role by their e-mail address.

Who may do what

  • For each process and form, a role can start items, only view them, or administer the flow. On forms, a role can edit or only read.
  • Examples: suppliers only read the help articles, and each RMES form is open only to its own department.

Inside each step

  • Each field is editable, read-only or hidden, per step. For example, the supplier edits their answers but can't change the material or the due date Beiersdorf set.
  • A step goes to a role (a department's review) or to people named in a field (the case's supplier contacts, or whoever raised an open point).
  • Each step can have a deadline with reminders. For example, "fill in the CIQ" is due on the supplier due date, with a reminder 7 days before and an overdue reminder the day after.

Reports

  • Every process comes with an "All items" report.
  • You can add your own reports, with your columns, filters and charts, and share each one with the roles that need it, such as the Business owner (reports).
