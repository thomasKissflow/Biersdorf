## 3. Blockers: your decisions before prod

### B-1 (P-1): the screens can't save after "fill in the CIQ". HIGH

**What happens:** both screens save through the user route (`POST /process/2/{acc}/{flow}/{item}/{activityInstance}`, the SDK's `updateItem`). Field-level step permissions make no difference on that route:
- **At "Supplier: fill in the CIQ"**, the only step where the Details section has a step permission, everything is writable. That includes fields you set ReadOnly:
  - App_Case_Item_Id, Release_Flag, Request_Locked and Case_Phase_Label were overwritten in all 10 runs;

  - the runner put every value back and read it back.
- **At every other step**, every write is refused with `KISSFLOW_ERROR_050313`, even for fields set Editable. That was 42 reproductions in pass 2 at:
  - "keep the CIQ up to date";
  - "give the final release";
  - "view the released CIQ";
  - the query's "respond";
